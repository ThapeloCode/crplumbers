About CR Plumbers & Electricians

Delivering dependable, code-compliant plumbing and electrical solutions across Pretoria North and surrounding Gauteng suburbs.

Who We Are

CR Plumbers & Electricians is a dual-trade contracting service based in Pretoria North (0182). We specialize in rapid emergency response and scheduled maintenance for residential, commercial, and industrial properties.

Whether dealing with a burst geyser at 2:00 AM or issuing an Electrical Certificate of Compliance (COC) for a property sale, our team approaches every task with strict adherence to South African compliance frameworks and SANS standards.

Why Property Owners Trust Us

24/7 Rapid Response

Plumbing leaks and DB board failures don't keep business hours. Our standby teams are positioned locally to ensure fast arrival times across Pretoria North.

Fully Licensed & Compliant

Our plumbing installations follow PIRB regulations, and all electrical interventions are carried out under registered wireman's licenses to protect your building insurance.

Transparent Pricing

No hidden fees or unexpected charges. We assess the situation, explain the required repair clearly, and provide upfront pricing before work begins.

Service Coverage Area

We actively service Pretoria North, Wolmer, Florauna, Ninapark, Tileba, Theresapark, Dorandia, Karenpark, Akasia, and broader northern Pretoria sectors.
